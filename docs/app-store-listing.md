### Description (≤4000)

Build a picture of your everyday habits with Turtle Steps to the Goal. Keep meals,
nutrition, weight and the things that matter to you together in a personal diary.
Choose a gentle pace and use your own history to understand changes over time.

YOUR EVERYDAY DIARY
Log meals, calories, protein, fat, carbohydrates, fiber and water. Add weight,
sleep, steps, mood, notes and body measurements. Record as much or as little as
you find useful, and create custom metrics for the things you want to observe.

FOODS AND RECIPES
Search the food catalog, save personal foods and build reusable recipes with
multiple ingredients. Scan a food barcode or enter it manually. Optional online
food lookup needs an internet connection.

YOUR PACE, YOUR HISTORY
Set weight-change goals and optional daily nutrition and water targets. Explore
charts, calendar history, summaries and correlations between your recorded
metrics. These views describe your entries; they do not establish causes or
provide medical advice.

BACKUP AND IMPORT
Import supported export files from Zepp Life, Apple Health and MyFitnessPal.
File import is separate from live health-app synchronization. Save a backup and
export your records as Excel, CSV or Markdown when you need them.

LOCAL STORAGE AND YOUR CHOICES
Your diary is stored on your device. There is no account to create, no developer
cloud diary and no analytics or telemetry. Make regular backups: uninstalling
the app or clearing its storage can remove your local records.

Exported files and foods or day logs you share through a QR code or link can
leave your device. Share only what you intend to disclose. Optional online
features contact external services; review the privacy policy for details.

Use the app in English or Russian, with light and dark modes and several color
themes. Turtle Steps is a personal tracking tool, not a medical device or a
substitute for professional care.

## App Privacy (“nutrition label”) — suggested answers

Answer from the **shipping** TestFlight/App Store build and the privacy policy,
not from this draft alone.

**Baseline (developer does not operate a diary backend):** the diary stays in
on-device storage; there is no account and no analytics SDK.

Suggested path if you treat optional network food lookup as user-initiated
contact with third parties (Open Food Facts / USDA) and not as your collection:

1. **Data Collection**: declare whether you (the developer) collect data.
   If nothing is transmitted to servers you control → often **Data Not Collected**.
2. Still review Apple’s questionnaire for:
   - **Health & Fitness** (weight, nutrition logged only on device)
   - **Product Interaction** / search if you conclude optional online food
     lookup must be disclosed as data leaving the device to third parties
   - **Diagnostics**: none if you send no crash/analytics telemetry
3. **Tracking**: **No** (no advertising identifiers / cross-app tracking).
4. Camera: used for barcode scanning; permission string already in Info.plist.
   Camera usage is a permission, not by itself a Privacy Nutrition Label type.

If App Store Connect wording is unclear, prefer the stricter disclosure that
matches what the binary can do (optional HTTPS food lookup and user exports)
over claiming “nothing ever leaves the device.”

### App Review reply missing / “no Reply button” (debug)

When App Review asks for Information Needed (e.g. Guideline 2.1), reply from
**App Review → submission → Reply to App Review** (attach screen recording
there). Also paste the same answers into **version → App Review Information →
Notes**.

If **Reply to App Review** is missing, **View** only loops version ↔ submission,
or passkey keeps offering the wrong email — check the **signed-in Apple ID
first** (2026-10-18):

1. **Wrong Apple ID (most common here).** The button only appears for
   **Account Holder / Admin / App Manager** on the team that owns the app.
   Turtle Steps is under **`zhannatravelzzz@gmail.com`**. Signing in as
   `zhanna.myshkovskaya@gmail.com` (or any other ID) can show the rejection
   message but **no Reply control**. Sign out → sign in with the team Apple ID.
   Passkey often re-offers the last saved key: **Cancel**, use password / a
   private window, or type the correct Apple ID instead of accepting the
   suggested passkey.
2. Stay on the **iOS Submission** page; scroll under **Messages** (Reply sits
   below the full Guideline text). Do not use **App Previews and Screenshots**
   for the review video — that tab is listing media only.
3. Confirm role under **Users and Access** if the correct Apple ID still has
   no Reply.
